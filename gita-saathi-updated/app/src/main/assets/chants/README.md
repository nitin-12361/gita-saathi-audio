# Real chanted/sung shloka audio (optional, per chapter)

Drop a real recording here named exactly:

    chapter_1.mp3
    chapter_2.mp3
    ...
    chapter_18.mp3

(one per chapter is realistic to source; per-verse would mean 700 separate files)

If a file for the chapter exists, the app plays it first (the real sung/chanted
shloka), then automatically continues into the Gemini-narrated meaning + modern
example. If it's missing, the app just skips straight to the narration — nothing
breaks.

## Where to get free/legal chant recordings
- Public-domain / Creative-Commons Sanskrit chanting on the Internet Archive
  (archive.org) — search "Bhagavad Gita chanting" and filter by license.
- Record your own with a priest/pandit or a singer you know — this is the most
  authentic option and what most competing Gita apps actually do.
- A royalty-free "Om chanting" or "Sanskrit shloka" track from a stock-audio
  library (Pixabay Audio, Free Music Archive) can work as a placeholder while you
  gather the real per-chapter recordings.

Format: mp3 or wav. Any sample rate — the app just plays it back as-is.
